# NexHeart Studio
### Thousands of files. One place to take control.
**Capture • Create • Play**

Your photos, videos, screenshots and notes deserve more than a maze of folders. NexHeart Studio brings media organization, screen capture, video editing, playback and useful Windows tools into one free desktop app — built for people with *thousands* of files.

**📂 Turn folders into visual libraries**
Browse your photos and videos in **PhotoAlbum**. Add a folder once — it's cached — and from then on it opens instantly, every time, even with thousands of items. Move, sort and organize files directly from the gallery. Spend less time opening files just to find out what they contain.

**🎬 See your videos before you open them**
Local videos get real frame previews within seconds — even where Windows shows a black thumbnail. In **VisionPlayer**, YouTube videos and playlists appear as thumbnail tiles with titles, not as a plain text list like most players.

**▶ Watch and download with VisionPlayer Pro**
Play local media, YouTube videos and playlists with VLC-powered playback, subtitles and selectable quality. Save YouTube videos or extract audio as MP3 — free, no limits, no ads — with playback and downloads in the same workspace.

**✂ Create with VideoEditor Pro**
Build videos on a timeline with splitting, effects, transitions, stickers, text, filters and custom watermarks. Export using presets for TikTok, Reels and YouTube. A CapCut-style editor with no subscription.

**📸 Capture it. Make it clear. Keep it handy.**
**QuickCapture**: one key for full-screen, window and region screenshots, scrolling captures, professional screen recording and a searchable capture history. **Smart IMG Pro** annotates, blurs and crops any image, extracts text (OCR) and reads QR codes.

**📝 Give your notes a home**
Keep your .txt notes, prompts and code snippets together in **TXTMaster**, organized with categories and bookmarks. One-click backup and restore carries your whole collection to the next Windows installation — reinstall and lose nothing.

**🔧 Prepare for your next Windows reinstall**
**Driver Nexus** inspects your devices, checks applicable driver updates through Windows Update and exports installed third-party driver packages as an offline pack for later reinstallation. Export diagnostic reports for troubleshooting, and use **Windows Install Prep** to get the machine ready before you wipe it.

**🧹 Make room for what matters**
Find duplicate files, large files and space-hungry folders with **Nexus Clean**. Review the selection and move unwanted files safely to the Recycle Bin.

**More in the same workspace:** AI Library · Resize Pro · Collages and slideshows · Photo TV and signage · 22 themes · Romanian and English interface · one-click updates

Built with Python, PyQt5, VLC and FFmpeg. Created by **AleXx626**.
